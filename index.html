History of hamburger

The hamburger can be traced to German cuisine. At least from the 17th century, the Hamburg steak, or Frikadelle, was a known delicacy in Germany. It consists of a ground beef patty and is lightly seasoned with salt. Related cousins are Salisbury and the French steak tartare. In other versions, the Hamburg steak was served with a bread roll, and it can be considered the origin of the hamburger.

German immigrants would bring German cuisine to the United States. In the early 19th century, German immigrants traveled to the New World from the port of Hamburg. Due to an increase in beef supplies and the use of mechanical shredders, the hamburger would become a cheap, popular meal by the end of the 19th century.
